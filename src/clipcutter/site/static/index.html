FAQ

Questions

How is it so fast on a 10-hour stream?

We don't download the stream. We read its captions, chat replay and YouTube's "most replayed" graph, which are just text, to find the best moments. Only the minute you turn into a clip is downloaded.

Which links work?

YouTube videos and live-stream VODs, Twitch VODs (twitch.tv/videos/…) and Kick VODs. Live streams can be clipped once the VOD is available.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Captions keep Hindi (Devanagari) text, and hooks are written in Hinglish for Hindi videos. English videos get English Gen Z hooks.

What counts as a clip?

Each clip you make uses one credit. Previewing moments is free. If a clip fails, the credit is refunded automatically. Credits reset every month.

Can I clip any creator's stream?

Only post clips you have the rights to: your own streams, or creators whose clipper programs allow it. Re-uploading other people's content without permission can get your account struck.

How long are clips kept?

7 days. Download them or share them straight from your phone.
